My Offers – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Barakah Innovations (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in the My Offers mobile application (“App”). My Offers helps users discover and interact with discounts, promotions, coupons, and offers.

Privacy contact: ela417894@gmail.com

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use My Offers, we may process the following information:

  • Account and authentication information: information needed to create or access an account, including information provided through Google sign-in or phone-based authentication.
  • Saved app information: favorites, wishlist items, alerts, and other preferences you choose to save in your account.
  • Usage information: information about interactions with offers, such as offer clicks and basic feature usage, to operate and improve the service.
  • AI feature usage: information you submit when using AI Search or AI Chat, together with limited usage information needed to apply service limits and operate these features.
  • Technical information: limited technical information that may be processed to provide security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and operate the App. Guest usage limits may use an IP address or similar technical identifier.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide and maintain My Offers;
  • authenticate users and maintain accounts;
  • save favorites, wishlists, alerts, and preferences;
  • provide AI Search and AI Chat;
  • display and manage offers, coupons, and related interactions;
  • apply usage limits and help prevent abuse or fraudulent activity;
  • diagnose technical problems, improve reliability, and maintain security; and
  • respond to support, privacy, and account-deletion requests.

3. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We may use service providers and technology partners that process information on our behalf when necessary to operate My Offers. Depending on the feature used, this may include authentication services, cloud/database infrastructure, AI service providers, and affiliate or offer-network services.

We do not sell your personal information as a product. We share information only when reasonably necessary to provide the service, protect the App and its users, comply with legal obligations, or otherwise as described in this Policy.

When you interact with an external merchant, affiliate network, coupon provider, or other third-party website from an offer, that third party may process information according to its own privacy policy and terms. You should review those policies before providing information to them.

4. AI Search and AI Chat

If you use AI Search or AI Chat, the content you submit may be processed by the AI infrastructure used to provide those features. We use this processing to generate responses and operate the AI functionality. Do not submit passwords, payment-card numbers, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information to AI features.

5. Location Information

My Offers may provide location-related offer features, such as nearby or physical-store information. The App is designed to request device location only when a location-based feature requires it and the user chooses to use that feature. We do not intentionally require continuous background location access for the normal operation of the App.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and fulfill the purposes described in this Policy. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it is processed.

8. Account and Data Deletion

If you have a My Offers account, you may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us at ela417894@gmail.com with the subject line My Offers Account Deletion Request.

We will process a valid deletion request within a reasonable period and delete associated personal data unless we are required or permitted by law to retain specific information for legitimate purposes such as security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. Any retained information will be limited to what is necessary for that purpose.

9. Children's Privacy

My Offers is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

10. International Processing

Service providers used by My Offers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we take reasonable steps to protect information during such processing.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted at the same public URL with a revised “Last updated” date.
